## What to submit

- A **CV** (maximum 3 pages).
- A **motivation letter** (maximum 1 page): say which position or positions you want and why. If you apply to more than one, give your order of preference.
- Your **BSc and MSc transcripts**.
- Optional but encouraged: a link to a code repository, or a one-page summary of a project or thesis.
- Contact details of **two referees**.

## Where to apply

There are two ways to apply:

- Each host institution will publish an **official vacancy** for its position. As these go live, we link them from the [position pages](/positions/).
- You can also apply now, or register your interest, through the **central AEGIS application portal**, which is linked on every [position page](/positions/). Name the position or positions you want.

First check that you meet the [eligibility rules](/job-requirements/).

## The process

1. **Screening** — we check eligibility and review your application.
2. **Interview** — an online interview, which may include a short discussion of your own work.
3. **Offer** — made by the host institution.

Applications are open now and reviewed as they arrive. Start dates run from February to September 2027.

## Equal opportunities

AEGIS follows open, transparent, merit-based selection, in line with the European Charter for Researchers. We especially welcome applications from women and from groups that are underrepresented in research. If you need a remote interview or any reasonable adjustment, tell us and we will arrange it.
