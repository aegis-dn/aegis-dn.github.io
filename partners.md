**[Technical University of Denmark (DTU)](https://www.dtu.dk/english/) — Denmark.** DTU Compute is the largest computer-science department in Denmark. AEGIS works with its Embedded Systems Engineering and Dynamical Systems sections and the Distributed Energy Systems section, which runs the SYSLAB and PowerLabDK smart-grid laboratories. DTU coordinates AEGIS.

**[École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) — Switzerland.** One of Europe's leading science and technology universities. Its Embedded Systems Laboratory leads the open hardware platform X-HEEP, and its POWERlab develops wide-band-gap power electronics and microfluidic chip cooling.

**[TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/en/) — Austria.** Austria's leading university for engineering and the natural sciences, with a top-ranked Informatics faculty. The host group works on distributed systems, cloud and edge computing, and energy-aware datacenter management.

**[TU Berlin](https://www.tu.berlin/en/) — Germany.** One of Germany's largest technical universities, known for AI, data science, and distributed systems. The host Chair of Software and Business Engineering works on edge AI and service-oriented, cloud, and fog computing.

**[Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) — United Kingdom.** A world-leading university for science, engineering, and business. Its Control and Power Research Group has a strong record in power engineering and large energy projects.

**[Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/) — Sweden.** Founded in 1666 and ranked among the world's top 100 universities. Its Department of Automatic Control at LTH is a world leader in control engineering and real-time computing.

**[Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en) — Finland.** A Finnish university across science, technology, economics, architecture, and design. Its Department of Information and Communications Engineering works on wireless communications, machine learning, and network technology.

**[Ericsson](https://www.ericsson.com/) — Finland.** Ericsson's Finnish research department has a long history in telecommunications and focuses on 5G/6G, the Internet of Things, and cloud, with a strong role in 3GPP and IETF standardization.

**[Dell Technologies](https://www.dell.com/) — Ireland.** A global technology company whose products include servers, storage, and datacenter hardware. The host group works on embedded AI in production server environments.

**[TDC NET](https://tdcnet.com/) — Denmark.** Denmark's leading telecommunications infrastructure provider, running the country's best-rated mobile network. It was the first company in the world with an SBTi-validated 2030 net-zero target.
