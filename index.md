## The challenge

Edge datacenters — small computing sites close to where data is produced — are spreading fast, driven by AI. But the power grid is under strain, and Europe wastes large amounts of clean energy because supply, demand, and grid limits do not match. Today, datacenters sit on the grid as passive loads, blind to its needs.

## The AEGIS solution

The same datacenters could help the grid instead. AEGIS builds software-defined flexibility: technologies that let a site change its power use on purpose — through batteries, cooling, and adaptable computing — and that coordinate many sites into one Virtual Power Plant. The work spans three layers: safe physical control, carbon-aware intelligence, and fleet orchestration. [Read about the research](/research/).
