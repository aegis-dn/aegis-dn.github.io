Before you apply, please check the rules below. They come from the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and apply to every position. Each [position](/positions/) also lists its own "Profile we are looking for" on its page.

## MSCA eligibility (you must meet these)

Document these in your application:

- **No doctoral degree.** At the date of recruitment you must not yet hold a PhD.
- **Mobility rule.** You must not have lived or done your main activity (work or studies) in the country of your host institution for more than 12 months in the 36 months just before you are recruited. Short stays such as holidays do not count.
- **All nationalities welcome.** The positions are open to applicants of any nationality.

## Your degree

You need an **MSc** (or a degree that lets you enrol in a PhD) in Computing, Electronics, Energy Systems, Telecommunications, or a related field. The degree must be finished by your start date — you can apply before you graduate.

## English

The work and the interviews are in English. If you have a test score (for example IELTS or TOEFL), include it; it is helpful but not always required.

## What we value

- A strong academic record.
- Programming, systems, or modeling skills that fit the position you choose.
- Initiative and curiosity.
- Good communication and teamwork.

Each [position](/positions/) lists the specific skills it needs, on top of these general points. When you are ready, read [how to apply](/how-to-apply/).
