AEGIS is organized in three research layers, which match its three work packages. Together they turn scattered edge datacenters into one carbon-efficient, grid-interactive resource. Each Doctoral Candidate (DC) owns one project; the projects are grouped by layer below.

## Layer 1 — Programmable infrastructure (WP1)

This layer gives safe, verifiable control over a site's physical parts: power electronics, cooling, and AI hardware. It moves step by step from safety guarantees, to switching flexibility on, to a model that reports how much flexibility is available.

- [DC1 — Grid Interface Control and Runtime Verification](/positions/dc01/)
- [DC2 — Grid-Interactive Thermal Flexibility for EDCs](/positions/dc02/)
- [DC3 — FPGA-Based AI Accelerator Options for Ultra-Low-Carbon Inference](/positions/dc03/)
- [DC4 — Carbon-Aware Digital Twins for Flexible EDCs](/positions/dc04/)

## Layer 2 — Carbon-aware intelligence (WP2)

This layer reads grid signals and turns high-level goals into concrete hardware and workload actions, without breaking service: forecasting, adaptable AI models, workload mobility, intent-driven control, and privacy and security.

- [DC5 — Privacy-Preserving Energy Flexibility Quantification for EDCs](/positions/dc05/)
- [DC6 — Flexibility Modeling and Forecasting for EDC Fleets](/positions/dc06/)
- [DC7 — Carbon-Aware Neural Architecture Search (NAS)](/positions/dc07/)
- [DC8 — Grid-Aware AI Workload Mobility for the Computing Continuum](/positions/dc08/)
- [DC9 — Intent-Driven Power Management and Facilities Control](/positions/dc09/)
- [DC10 — Privacy and Safety for Carbon-Aware Site-Level Workload Management](/positions/dc10/)

## Layer 3 — Fleet orchestration (WP3)

This layer coordinates hundreds of sites into one Virtual Power Plant: end-to-end energy models, a cryptographic trust layer, fleet-level workload shaping, developer tools, and a live telecom pilot.

- [DC11 — End-to-End Energy Modeling for Distributed AI Services](/positions/dc11/)
- [DC12 — Agentic Verification for Trusted Autonomous Operations](/positions/dc12/)
- [DC13 — Fleet-Level Workload Shaping with Live Grid Signals](/positions/dc13/)
- [DC14 — Energy-Efficient Full-Stack Development Toolchain for Energy-Aware AI Apps](/positions/dc14/)
- [DC15 — Responsive Telecom Edge Sites Aggregated as Virtual Power Plants](/positions/dc15/)

## Where we test the platform

AEGIS proves its technologies in two real settings:

- A **hybrid-workload telecom edge fleet**, where telecom network functions and general AI workloads share the same sites.
- **Carbon-aware multi-tenant edge datacenters**, where several tenants share one site and security must be kept while saving carbon.
