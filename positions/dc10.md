## The project

A datacenter that hosts many tenants must keep them strictly separated, but that separation costs energy — and nobody knows how much. Because the cost is unknown, security is left out of carbon-aware planning. In this project you measure it and bring it in.

You will measure the energy and performance cost of state-of-the-art isolation mechanisms, turn that into machine-readable security policies, and build a site-level scheduler that balances tenant service levels, security, and energy use together.

**What you will work on:**

- Measure the energy and performance overhead of state-of-the-art tenant-isolation mechanisms.
- Develop machine-readable security policies that describe the trade-off between isolation level and energy cost.
- Create a site-level scheduler that co-optimizes tenant service levels, security, and energy use.

**What you will deliver:**

- A catalog of energy and performance overheads for common isolation technologies.
- An open-source library that generates and enforces energy-aware security policies.
- A site-level scheduling prototype that balances security, performance, and carbon in a simulated multi-tenant datacenter.
