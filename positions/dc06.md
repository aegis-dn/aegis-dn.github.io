## The project

Carbon-aware control can only plan ahead if it has good forecasts of future grid conditions. In this project you build the forecasting engine for a fleet of edge datacenters.

You will extend a Smart Energy operating system developed at DTU with forecasts of carbon intensity, energy prices, and grid congestion across many time horizons, together with the uncertainty estimates that risk-aware scheduling needs. You will also combine the limits of many sites into fleet-wide flexibility functions.

**What you will work on:**

- Produce multi-horizon forecasts for carbon, prices, and congestion, from five minutes to a day ahead.
- Develop flexibility-function representations that compress a site's constraints into a compact form.
- Design a hierarchical method that adds up flexibility across the whole fleet.

**What you will deliver:**

- A forecasting suite for grid signals with measured accuracy and calibrated uncertainty bounds.
- An aggregation framework, with a report on its economic value in simulated market scenarios.
