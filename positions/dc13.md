## The project

In this project you build the brain of the fleet: the central engine that turns many separate sites into one carbon-efficient grid resource.

Reacting to live grid signals, the orchestrator makes two kinds of decision: workload shaping (where to place, pause, or move work) and flexibility dispatch (which sites should change their power using batteries, cooling, and adaptable compute). It must scale to hundreds of sites and act across timescales from sub-second to hourly.

**What you will work on:**

- Design a multi-agent orchestrator that manages 100 to 1000 sites.
- Implement multi-timescale control, from sub-second to hourly.
- Develop contextual bandits with bounded-regret guarantees.

**What you will deliver:**

- An orchestration prototype with measured decision latency and scalability across various fleet sizes.
- A counterfactual analysis toolkit that estimates the carbon savings of different orchestration policies.
