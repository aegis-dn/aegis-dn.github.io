Dell Technologies is a global technology company whose products include servers, storage, datacenter hardware, and security services. AEGIS works with the CTO Research group (OCTO) in Cork, Ireland.

**Location:** Cork, Ireland  
**Role in AEGIS:** Hosts DC10 (degree awarded by Imperial College London).  
**Website:** [www.dell.com](https://www.dell.com/)
