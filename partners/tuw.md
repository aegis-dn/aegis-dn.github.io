TU Wien is Austria's leading university for engineering and the natural sciences. Its Informatics faculty is ranked first in Austria and among the best in Europe.

**Location:** Vienna, Austria  
**Role in AEGIS:** Hosts DC4.  
**Website:** [www.tuwien.at/en](https://www.tuwien.at/en/)
