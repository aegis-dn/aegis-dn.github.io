Imperial College London is a world-leading university for science, technology, engineering, medicine, and business. AEGIS works with the Control and Power Research Group in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

**Location:** London, United Kingdom  
**Role in AEGIS:** Hosts DC5; awards the degree for DC10.  
**Website:** [www.imperial.ac.uk](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/)
