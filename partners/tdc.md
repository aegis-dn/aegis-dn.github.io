TDC NET runs Denmark's leading telecommunications infrastructure across copper, coax, fiber, and mobile networks. It operates the country's best-rated mobile network and was the first company in the world with an SBTi-validated 2030 net-zero target, with a strong interest in the energy use of mobile networks.

**Location:** Denmark  
**Role in AEGIS:** Hosts DC15 (degree awarded by DTU).  
**Website:** [tdcnet.com](https://tdcnet.com/)
