Ericsson Research Finland has a long history of research and development in telecommunications and currently focuses on 5G/6G, the Internet of Things, and cloud-native products, with a strong record in 3GPP and IETF standardization.

**Location:** Finland  
**Role in AEGIS:** Hosts DC14 (degree awarded by Aalto University).  
**Website:** [www.ericsson.com](https://www.ericsson.com/)
