The Technical University of Denmark (DTU) is a leading European technical university and the coordinator of AEGIS. DTU takes part with three departments and four research groups: DTU Compute (the Embedded Systems Engineering and Dynamical Systems groups), DTU Wind and Energy Systems (the Distributed Energy Systems group), and DTU Electro (the Network Technologies group), which hosts the PhD enrolment for the Doctoral Candidate based at TDC NET (DC15).

**Location:** Lyngby, Denmark  
**Role in AEGIS:** Coordinator; hosts DC1, DC6, DC7, DC13; awards the degree for DC15.  
**Website:** [www.dtu.dk/english](https://www.dtu.dk/english/)
