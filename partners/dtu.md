The Technical University of Denmark (DTU) is a leading European technical university and the coordinator of AEGIS. DTU takes part with two departments and three research groups, together with a fourth group at DTU Electro that hosts one PhD enrolment.

**Location:** Lyngby, Denmark  
**Role in AEGIS:** Coordinator; hosts DC1, DC6, DC7, DC13; awards the degree for DC15.  
**Website:** [www.dtu.dk/english](https://www.dtu.dk/english/)

## Groups and researchers

_This page is maintained by the beneficiary. Researchers: feel free to update your description and add a personal web page next to your ORCID._

### Embedded Systems Engineering (ESE)
*DTU Compute*

Methods, tools, and architectures for dependable edge computing, edge AI, and low-energy computing.

- **Prof. Paul Pop** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9981-1775)) — Professor of Cyber-Physical Systems and Head of ESE; AEGIS Coordinator. Supervises DC13; co-supervises DC15. Coordinated the FORA MSCA network (2017–2021).
- **Prof. Xenofon Fafoutis** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9871-0013)) — Professor of Networked Embedded Systems; embedded AI and energy-efficient machine learning. Supervises DC7; co-supervises DC3.
- **Dr. Luca Pezzarossa** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0863-2526)) — Researcher in embedded systems and time-predictable platforms.

### Dynamical Systems (DynSys)
*DTU Compute*

Modeling, optimization, forecasting, and control of energy systems, data centers, and smart cities.

- **Prof. Henrik Madsen** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0690-3713)) — Leading expert in smart-energy systems and stochastic dynamical systems. Supervises DC6.

### Distributed Energy Systems (DES)
*DTU Wind and Energy Systems*

Smart grids and data-driven control of distributed energy resources; runs the SYSLAB and PowerLabDK laboratories.

- **Henrik Bindner** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1545-6994)) — Senior Researcher and Head of DES; sector-coupled control of distributed energy resources. Supervises DC1; co-supervises DC5.
- **Dr. Tilman Weckesser** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7541-2283)) — Researcher in power-system dynamics and control.
- **Kai Heussen** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3623-1372)) — Researcher in power-system control and smart-grid laboratory automation.

### Network Technologies
*DTU Electro*

Green cellular communications and 5G/6G; hosts the PhD enrolment for the Doctoral Candidate based at TDC NET (DC15).

- **Assoc. Prof. Michael Stübert Berger** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4575-7261)) — Expert in green cellular communications and 5G/6G; co-supervises and hosts the degree for DC15.
