AEGIS will recruit **15 Doctoral Candidates (DCs)** — fully funded, 36-month PhD positions at leading European universities and companies. Each position has its own research project, host, and pair of secondments. You can see all 15 below.

Applications open in **autumn 2026** and are reviewed as they arrive. We aim to fill every position by **April 2027**, and the latest start date is **September 2027**, so that all Doctoral Candidates can attend the first training school.

## What we offer

- A salary set by the EU Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions: a **living allowance of about €4,010 per month** (adjusted by a country correction factor for your host country), a **mobility allowance of €710 per month**, and, if you qualify, a **family allowance of €660 per month**. These are gross EU contributions; your employer deducts taxes and social charges, so the exact net salary depends on the host country.
- A full employment contract with social security, not a stipend.
- Enrolment in a PhD program at a top university.
- Two funded secondments inside the network — one in industry, one in academia.
- A travel and research budget, and a network-wide training program (see [Why join AEGIS?](/dn-benefits/)).

## Who we are looking for

You have, or are close to finishing, an **MSc in Computing, Electronics, Energy Systems, Telecommunications, or a related field**. The projects cover computer systems and cloud, power systems and energy informatics, optimization and control, and machine learning and data science. Read the full [eligibility and profile](/job-requirements/).
